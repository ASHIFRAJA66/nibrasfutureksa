FAQ

Questions customers ask

All questions →
Do you repair all makes?

Yes. We repair machines and engines of every make, such as Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hyundai, Doosan, Volvo, JCB and others.

Do you sell spare parts?

No, we are a repair workshop only. We fit parts that you bring, and we make some parts on the lathe, such as pins and bushings.

Do you come to the machine's site?

Yes for smaller jobs, in Riyadh and the surrounding area. Bigger jobs such as overhauls are done in the workshop, where the result is better.

How do I know the cost?

We inspect the machine first, then give you a written quotation before starting. We don't start any work without your approval.
