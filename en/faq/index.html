Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about repairs, cost, warranty and on-site work.

WhatsApp us 055 967 0724
Do you repair all makes?

Yes. We repair machines and engines of every make, such as Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hyundai, Doosan, Volvo, JCB and others.

Do you sell spare parts?

No, we are a repair workshop only. We fit parts that you bring, and we make some parts on the lathe, such as pins and bushings.

Do you come to the machine's site?

Yes for smaller jobs, in Riyadh and the surrounding area. Bigger jobs such as overhauls are done in the workshop, where the result is better.

How do I know the cost?

We inspect the machine first, then give you a written quotation before starting. We don't start any work without your approval.

Is there a warranty on the work?

Warranty depends on the type of work and parts. When a job carries a warranty, we write it on the job card.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the job and parts availability. We write the expected ready date on the job card and message you on WhatsApp when the machine is ready.

Can I bring just the part, without the machine?

Yes. You can bring the cylinder, engine, pin or any part, and we repair it in the workshop.

How do I follow my machine's progress?

Every machine with us gets a tag with a QR code. Scan it with your phone to see the job card: date received, the problem and the work done.

Do you issue tax invoices?

Yes. The company is VAT registered. Our VAT number is 314591540700003.

Do you offer maintenance contracts for companies?

Yes. We agree with the company on the machine list, how we work and pricing. More details on the companies page.

What are your working hours?

Saturday to Thursday: 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and 2:00 PM to 7:30 PM. Closed on Friday.

How do I send photos of the problem?

Send them on WhatsApp to 0559670724, or fill in the repair request form on the site, then attach the photos in the same WhatsApp chat.
