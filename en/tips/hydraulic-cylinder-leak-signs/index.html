5 signs your hydraulic cylinder needs resealing

How to spot early that an excavator or loader cylinder needs a new seal kit, and why waiting costs more.

Nebras Afaq Al-Mustaqbal workshop · Riyadh

The seals in a cylinder stop oil leaking and keep the pressure inside. Daily work, heat and dust wear them out, and if they aren't replaced in time the rod or barrel itself can be damaged.

Signs to watch for

  • Oil on the rod or around the cylinder head after work.
  • The boom or bucket slowly drifting down while the machine is parked.
  • Weak lifting, especially with a heavy load.
  • Scratches, pitting or rust on the chrome rod.
  • Jerky movement or unusual noise when the cylinder moves.

Why not wait?

A small leak lets dust into the cylinder, which scores the rod and barrel. Then a seal kit is no longer enough, and the rod may need repair or replacement, which costs far more.

Tip: clean mud and dust off the rod after work, and don't leave the machine parked for long periods with a cylinder fully extended in the sun.

These are general tips; always follow the manufacturer's manual for your machine.
